## What was built
A mass on a spring, with optional damping (a brake that grows with speed) and a sinusoidal
push. In the proper terms: `m x'' + b x' + k x = F0 cos(wt)`, solved exactly and numerically
(SciPy), with the two compared on every run. The endpoint is `POST /api/v1/physics/oscillator/simulate`
and the page is `/physics/oscillator`. Plan: `docs/plans/ROADMAP_2_3_PLAN.md`; guide: Part 20.

## What it shows, and how we know
| Claim | How it was checked | Label |
|---|---|---|
| The exact solution is correct in every damping regime, including critical damping | the ODE residual, initial conditions and identities (peak ratio `e^{-gamma T_d}`, half-power width `2 gamma`) in `test_oscillator_analytical.py`, against a 90-digit reference | Proved (closed forms), Tested (floating point) |
| Q = w0/(width of the power peak) exactly; Q ~ 2 pi x stored/lost per cycle only approximately | closed form (the width 2 gamma is exact); the approximate form is labelled in the guide and SCIENTIFIC_VALIDATION.md, not shown by the app | Proved; approximate |
| The numerical solve agrees with the exact one | displacement, velocity and energy-balance checks per run; thresholds from 1608 measured runs on one machine | Tested; thresholds Observed (not a bound) |
| A wrong damping law is caught | mutation test: `-b v \|v\|/V` fails the energy balance and displacement checks | Tested |
| Limits keep a run accurate and fast: Q <= 1e4 when b > 0, 500 cycles, run-cost budget of 400 000 right-hand-side calls | measured against a 90-digit reference and by timing (one machine) | Observed |
| The page shows the backend's numbers, labels each by origin, and shows undefined ones as absent, never 0 | Vitest (270 passed in the slice-4 tick) and Playwright (7 passed, 5 + 2) | Tested |

## One figure to reproduce
Page `/physics/oscillator`, preset **Driven**: m = 1 kg, k = 25 N/m, b = 0.5 kg/s, F0 = 1 N,
w = 5 rad/s (= sqrt(k/m)), x0 = v0 = 0. Look for the steady amplitude `F0/(b w0) = 0.4 m`
(formula, tested on the page), a phase lag of pi/2, and the resonance chart with this run near
the peak of the curve (the peak is at w_r, slightly below w0).

## Two-minute demo
1. Open the page from the nav bar; press **Simple**, Run: a steady swing, energy swapping.
2. Press **Damped**, Run: the dotted envelope shrinks the swings; Q is shown.
3. Press **Driven**, Run: point at the resonance chart and say "pushing near the natural
   frequency makes the response largest; the lag is a quarter cycle".
4. Under Validation, show the checks against the exact solution, and say they are measured
   thresholds, not a proof. Set b = 0: Q is absent, not 0.
